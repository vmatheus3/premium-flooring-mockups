Common
questions.
Good decisions start with
knowing what to expect.
Ask Andre
Can my floor be refinished?
Usually, if the boards are solid wood or thick enough engineered wood. Andre checks the wear and past sanding at the visit.
Refinish or replace?
If the boards are sound, refinishing is usually the simpler option. We will tell you honestly at the visit.
How long does it take?
It depends on the size, stain and finish. You will get a timeline with your written estimate.
Will there be dust?
Sanding makes dust. We will explain how we contain it and clean up.
When can furniture go back?
It depends on the finish. Andre will tell you walking, furniture and rug timing.
