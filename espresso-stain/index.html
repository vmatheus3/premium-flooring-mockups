FAQ

Can my floor be refinished?

Usually, if the boards are solid wood or thick enough engineered wood; Andre checks the wear and past sanding at the visit.

Refinish or replace?

If the boards are sound, refinishing is usually the simpler option; we'll tell you honestly at the visit.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size, stain and finish; you'll get a timeline with your written estimate.

Will there be dust?

Sanding makes dust; we'll explain how we contain it and clean up.

When can furniture go back?

It depends on the finish; Andre will tell you walking, furniture and rug timing.
